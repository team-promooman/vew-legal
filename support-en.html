A problem with an offer

The fastest route is inside the app: open the offer, scroll to the bottom and tap Report offer. Choose whether it has expired, the price is wrong, or the item is unavailable. Reports reach the team directly and are reviewed against the store.

Remember that offers belong to the stores. We publish what they give us and correct it when it turns out to be wrong.

Your account and your data

  • Change city or gender — Account screen, at any time.
  • Delete your account — Account screen → Delete account. What happens is set out in the Privacy Policy.
  • Request a copy of your data — write to us; we answer within 30 days.

Trouble signing in

The SMS code can take up to a minute. Check the number is an Omani mobile starting with 7 or 9, and that you have signal. If it still does not arrive, contact us with the number you are trying and we will check it from our side.

For stores

If you run a store in Oman and want your offers in the app, get in touch through the channels above. Tell us your store name, the cities you operate in, and the kind of products you sell.
