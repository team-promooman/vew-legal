A problem with an offer

The fastest route is inside the app: open the offer, scroll to the bottom and tap Report offer. Choose whether it has expired, the price is wrong, or the item is unavailable. Reports reach the team directly and are reviewed against the store.

Remember that offers belong to the stores. We publish what they give us and correct it when it turns out to be wrong.

Your account and your data

  • Change city or gender — Account screen, at any time.
  • Request a copy of your data — write to us; we answer within 30 days.

Deleting your account

In the app: Account screen → Delete account. You are shown exactly what goes, and nothing happens until you confirm.

Without the app: email support@vew-om.com from the address your account uses, with “Delete my account” as the subject. If you signed in with Apple and chose to hide your email, write from that private relay address — it is the address we hold, and it reaches us. We delete the account once we can match it, and reply when it is done.

What is deleted: your profile, your favourites and your activity history. Deletion begins as soon as it is confirmed and finishes within 48 hours at the outside. If you sign in again and choose a city before it finishes, the deletion is cancelled.

Anonymised counts that carry no identifier — how many people saw a given offer — are not tied to you and are not removed by this. The full account is in the Privacy Policy.

Trouble signing in

Browsing needs no account: tap Enter as a visitor on the first screen and the whole catalogue opens. An account is only needed to save favourites and to report an offer.

To create one, use Continue with Apple. If the button does not appear on your device, sign-in is not available there yet — browsing still is, in full. Contact us if the button is there and does not complete, and tell us the device and what you saw.

For stores

If you run a store in Oman and want your offers in the app, get in touch through the channels above. Tell us your store name, the cities you operate in, and the kind of products you sell.
